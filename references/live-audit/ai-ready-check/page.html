Honest note

  • This page reports the public access rules and markup that a crawler would encounter. It does not determine whether any AI platform will index, use or cite your site — those are separate decisions made by each platform.
  • The score is a fixed, readable checklist, not a prediction. It is computed here in plain code, with no AI model involved.
  • llms.txt is an optional emerging convention, not a universal requirement. Missing it is not a fault.
  • This is an AI Factory experiment. It makes four requests, stores nothing, needs no account, and sets no cookies.
